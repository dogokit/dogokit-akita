# SignuDoc

SignuDoc is an open-source e-signature app that you host yourself, on your own Vercel account. You upload a PDF, place the signing fields, send signing links, and keep the signed copy with its audit certificate. It is an alternative to paid e-signature services like DocuSign, and it is not affiliated with DocuSign.
