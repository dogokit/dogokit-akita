# Pact

Open-source contract testing for APIs and messages, with PactFlow as the hosted version of its broker.

Pact checks that a client and a service agree on the shape of their calls before either one ships. The client writes a contract from its own tests, and the service checks it. PactFlow is the commercial, managed Pact Broker that stores and shares those contracts, now owned by SmartBear.
