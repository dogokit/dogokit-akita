# Fish Audio

Fish Audio is an AI voice platform. It turns text into speech, clones a voice from a short sample, and turns speech into text. It is here as an AI audio tool for voice output

Official links:

- [Fish Audio](https://fish.audio): official site
- [Fish Audio Docs](https://docs.fish.audio): official docs
- [Fish Audio GitHub](https://github.com/fishaudio): source code and SDKs
- Package: `fish-audio` on npm (JavaScript) and `fish-audio-sdk` on PyPI (Python)
