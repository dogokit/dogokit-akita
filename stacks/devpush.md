# devpush

devpush is an open-source deployment platform for Docker Compose. Connect GitHub, and it runs your apps, APIs, workers and databases on your own Linux servers. It is here as a self-host option next to [Coolify](coolify.md) and [Dokku](dokku.md)

- [devpush](https://devpu.sh): official site
- [devpush Docs](https://devpu.sh/docs/): official docs
